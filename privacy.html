Privacy Policy

This site collects very little. Here is all of it, what happens to it, and how to get it back or deleted.

Who we are

Spartan Mechatronics LLC, South Carolina, USA. Questions about this page go to info@spartanmechatronics.com.

What the quote form collects

Only what you type into it:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name, if you add it (it's optional)
  • Your message about the job

Along with the form, the page sends its own address and title so we know which page the request came from.

Where it goes and why

When you press Send, the form goes to HubSpot, the service we use to keep track of quote requests and email, and a copy is emailed to us through Google. We use it only to reply to your request, send the quote and handle any work that follows. We don't sell it, rent it or share it with anyone else for their own use.

Phone calls

When you call (843) 806-0488, it rings Mark first. If he can't pick up, an AI assistant answers and takes a message: your name, company, callback number, email and what's going on with the machine. The call runs through Twilio (the phone service) and ElevenLabs (the AI voice), which keep a transcript and recording of the call. The message then goes to HubSpot and is emailed to us through Google, the same as the quote form. We use it only to call you back about the job.

Fonts from Google

The lettering on this site loads from Google Fonts. To send the fonts, Google's servers receive your IP address and basic browser details, the same as for any file your browser asks for. Google's own privacy policy covers what they do with it.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ad or tracking scripts. Like any web host, the service that serves these pages (GitHub Pages) may keep short-lived server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages asked for, to keep the site running and secure.

How long we keep it

We keep a quote request and the emails that follow while we're talking about the job and while any work from it is under way. After that we keep it for up to three years after our last contact, for records and follow-up questions, and then delete it. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will.

Your copy, your changes, deletion

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email the address below and say which one you want. We'll reply within 30 days. If we have to keep anything for legal or tax reasons, we'll tell you what and why.

Data deletion request: email info@spartanmechatronics.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the email address you used on the form.

Changes to this page

If what we collect or how we use it changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
